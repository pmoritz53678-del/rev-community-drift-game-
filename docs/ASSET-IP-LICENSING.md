# Asset provenance, trademarks, Unreal license and publishing

## Originality
GTA-style **world-building** here means original fictional manufacturers and model families that communicate *vehicle class*, not renamed exact clones. Even without logos, near-identical bodywork can create design/trade-dress risks. Original headlights, grille, tail lamps, shoulder lines, cockpit, aerodynamic surfaces and proportions must be reviewed. Do not import GTA, Torque Drift, manufacturer or paid Marketplace models into this public repository.

Our first model generator uses *simple geometric archetypes only*. The OBJ files are design scaffolding, not final vehicle art. Before marketing, do a 360-degree resemblance audit with visual references.

## Engine and third-party tools
- Download/install the Unreal Engine editor via Epic's launcher: https://www.unrealengine.com/download
- Unreal Engine itself is **not** redistributed in this repository. Its license and third-party content license apply separately.
- A royalty-bearing commercial Unreal game generally triggers Epic's 5% royalty **after its first $1M USD of lifetime product gross revenue**, subject to Epic's exclusions, reporting requirements and any later changes. Read the live agreement before shipping: https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/eula/unreal
- Marketplace/Fab assets each have their own rights and may not permit extraction or public repo redistribution. Keep proof of license and attribution in an internal asset ledger.
- Add an asset row (creator, source, license, acquisition date, redistribution permission, derivative status) before committing an externally authored model, font, sound, texture or plugin.

## App Store
- Apple rule 4.1 rejects copycat or impersonator apps. Unique design/gameplay and marketing are required. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Check Android/iOS SDK requirements, store age rating, app privacy disclosures, music/audio permissions and consumer rules before launch.
- No real manufacturer or celebrity likeness without appropriate permission. Do not imply official affiliation to any automaker or to Epic.
- Seek legal review on trade dress and branding before commercial distribution. This document is not legal advice.
