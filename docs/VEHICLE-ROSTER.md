## Design language to distinguish originals
- **Kairo**: offset horizontal headlights, upright wingless tails, a folded shoulder crease and simple seven-spoke wheel
- **Hinode**: long sloping rear glass, rising lower sill, tapered triangular nose
- **Alden**: long hood, trapezoid grille, broad convex fenders and rectangular rear lamps
- **Valenza**: aggressive side intakes, sculptural rear flying buttress, bespoke split front lamps
- **Northline**: tall glasshouse, slim light bar, estate roof
- **Satori**: recessed cockpit, slim round-rectangle lights and flat deck
- **Aerion**: futuristic low bubble canopy, detached aero fins; no manufacturer trademarks
- **Sora**: compact rear-drive coupe with one-piece rear glass and narrow lamps
- **Velasco**: sculpted open roadster with separate roll hoops

### Approval gate
Before shipping, compare front, side and rear renders to the closest known production cars. Alter recognizable protected design signatures (bumper/light arrangements, vents, spoilers, grille, silhouette specifics); original branding by itself is **not** enough to avoid IP/trade-dress risk. No licensed name/logo or exact manufacturer identification without an agreement.
